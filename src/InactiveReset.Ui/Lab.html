What this reset restores

Pose: selected recorded position and orientation.
Speed / gear: eligible motion only; standing teleport skips them.
Chassis rotation: speed reset starts with zero angular motion.
Wheel rotation: speed reset computes it from speed and current wheel radius; recorded wheel rates are not restored.
Suspension: recorded deflections, ride heights and loads are not restored. Speed reset keeps the suspension configuration present at reset time.
Tyres: current tyres by default. Optional overrides apply through the garage Drive handoff.
Fuel, energy, damage and session clock: not restored.
